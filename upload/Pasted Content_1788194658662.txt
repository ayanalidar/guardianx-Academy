# 1. CORE BUSINESS POSITIONING

GuardianX Academy is NOT primarily a generic self-paced course marketplace.

The core model is:

# LEARN → TRAIN → PRACTICE → PREPARE → CERTIFY → ADVANCE

GuardianX provides instructor-led cybersecurity education through structured training batches, hands-on labs, study materials, assessments, workshops, CTFs and institutional programs.

# 8. CERTIFICATION BATCH MODEL

A certification is NOT the same thing as a course.

This distinction is critical.

Example:

### Certification

CompTIA Security+

may have:

* Certification information
* Exam objectives
* Skills
* Training course
* Multiple batches
* Different schedules
* Different instructors

Therefore:

```text
CERTIFICATION
      ↓
TRAINING PROGRAM
      ↓
BATCH
      ↓
LIVE SESSIONS
      ↓
LEARNING MATERIAL
      ↓
LABS
      ↓
MOCK TESTS
      ↓
EXAM PREPARATION
```

# 31. COURSE VS BATCH

Do not confuse these.

### COURSE

Reusable educational content.

### BATCH

Scheduled delivery of a course/training program.

One course can have many batches.

Example:

```text
COURSE
"Ethical Hacking Fundamentals"

        ↓

BATCH 01
Weekday Evening

BATCH 02
Weekend Morning

BATCH 03
Weekend Evening

BATCH 04
Late Night
```

This architecture is essential.
